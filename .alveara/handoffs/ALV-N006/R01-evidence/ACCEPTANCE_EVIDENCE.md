# ALV-N006 R01 - Acceptance evidence

The seven acceptance items are the "Acceptance / stop condition" bullets of item 53 in `GPT Docs/Alveara_Dental_Execution_Plan_v4.md`. Test names are in `src/Alveara.Api.Tests/` and `src/alveara-client/src/`; the walkthrough is `src/alveara-client/e2e/reports-real-backend.spec.ts`; behaviour is documented in `docs/REPORTING.md`. Not proven at Stage 1: the full backend regression (Stage 2).

1. **Each first-release report category is represented.** Ten reports in four groups, each with its own permission: operational (schedule, patient flow, recall and follow-up, cancellations and no-shows), financial (production and charges, collections and payments, balances), clinical (treatment-plan status, completed procedures, provider activity) and measurement (metrics, baselines, comparison). Tests: `OperationalReportsTests` 14, `FinancialReportsTests` 12, `BalancesReportTests` 8, `ClinicalReportsTests` 10, `ProviderActivityTests` 7, `FollowUpReportTests` 7, `CancellationReportTests` 6, `MetricsTests` 6, `MeasurementBaselineTests` 11; the hub lists each report to the roles that hold it (`ReportsHubPage.test.tsx` 18, `OperationalViews.test.tsx` 13, `FinancialClinicalViews.test.tsx` 26 including one case per view); walkthrough ACCESS shows front desk Operational, billing Financial, dentist Clinical, office manager all four groups.
2. **Financial totals reconcile to ledger source data.** Every ledger entry is classified into exactly one line (ten lines including loaded opening balances and an always-empty "unclassified"), and the lines are shown beside an independent sum of the same entries with a sentence that says whether they agree (a warning when not). Tests: `FinancialReportsTests` (lines equal the ledger and an independent sum; opening balance apart from production and collections; provider amendment moves production; every drill-down total equals its cell), `BalancesReportTests` (start plus change equals end, reconciled to the ledger), `ReportScaleTests` (5,000 entries on 20 accounts: payments received 49,800.00 and net balance as inserted, ledger proof reconciled). Walkthrough COLLECTIONS: the screen shows $40.00 and the proof sentence; independent SQL gives 40.00. Negative controls 6 and 7 are caught.
3. **Patient-flow counts reconcile to visit states.** The patient-flow report counts appointments by visit state and by transition and carries a `reconciled` flag; the schedule and cancellations reports reconcile to the same appointments. Tests: `OperationalReportsTests` (parts add up to the whole, drill-down totals equal cells, state and transition counts), `CancellationReportTests` (6), `ReportScaleTests` (4,380 appointments: schedule, cancellations and the CSV equal what was inserted). Walkthrough SCHEDULE: 5 appointments, 2 cancelled, 3 scheduled on screen and by an independent SQL count; pressing the cancelled count lists exactly those two.
4. **Baseline metrics show supporting counts and records and never fabricate missing history.** Each of the seven measured metrics shows what was counted out of what with its definition version; a metric with nothing in its denominator shows "Not available", never 0%; the three things the application cannot observe (time entering the same information twice, administrative time, the overhead reduction) are listed with the reason and never given a number, and the 20 to 30 percent reduction is stated as a target. A baseline is a snapshot of a period that is already over, stored with each metric's definition version and immutable (database triggers). Metrics that have records link to them (cancellations, no-shows, follow-ups). Tests: `MetricsTests` (values checked by hand and pinned to the definition versions; the pins now cover every reversal kind), `MeasurementBaselineTests` (11: immutability, idempotent save, a period that is not over is refused), `MeasurementApiTests` (7), `MeasurementView.test.tsx` (17). Walkthrough MEASUREMENT: cancellations 2 of 5 equal the SQL count, a future-period baseline is refused and writes nothing, a past one is saved once (1 baseline, 7 stored metrics, 1 audit entry). Negative controls 4 and 5 are caught.
5. **Improvement percentages appear only when a valid comparable baseline exists.** A change is shown for a metric only if the baseline period ended before this one started, the metric has the same definition version in both and both denominators hold at least 30 records; otherwise the metric shows its own value, the reason in words and "No change shown". A valid change is worded Higher, Lower or The same, by percentage points and as a share of the baseline rate, never as an improvement, and every comparison carries the sentence that a difference does not show a cause. Tests: `MeasurementBaselineTests` (each refusal reason; no change fields unless comparable), `MeasurementApiTests`, `MeasurementView.test.tsx` (the no-baseline state, a rise, a fall and an equal rate, the refused rows, no word calling a change good or bad). Walkthrough: the comparison shows "No change shown" with a "too few records" reason and neither the page nor the CSV calls a change good or bad. Not shown in the walkthrough: a comparable change (it needs 30 or more records in both periods; the unit and client tests cover it). Negative controls 4 and 9 are caught.
6. **No-data and large-range states are honest and usable.** A range with nothing in it says "Nothing to report" instead of a screen of zeros; a range over 366 days is refused with a stable code and recorded nowhere; a report that would read more than 200,000 source rows and an export of more than 50,000 lines are refused, never cut short; the read is one snapshot when the database allows it and the report says how it was read. Tests: `OperationalReportsTests` (limit exact: at the limit runs, one over is refused), `FinancialReportsTests`, `ClinicalReportsTests`, `FollowUpReportTests` (size refusals), `ReportCsvTests` (the export limit at its boundary), `ReportScaleTests` (a full year answers each report with a small fixed number of database commands, none takes a minute, the slowest 0.116 s; a range over the limit is refused with `range_too_large` and records nothing). Walkthrough HONEST STATES: "Nothing to report" and the range refusal in words, with screenshots. Negative control 8 is caught.
7. **Restricted users cannot access unauthorized financial or clinical detail.** Each group has its own permission (`ViewOperationalReports`, `ViewFinancialReports`, `ViewClinicalReports`, `ViewMeasurementReports`, `ManageMeasurementBaselines`); the server checks it on every call including drill-downs and exports, refuses a filter a report cannot honestly apply, and reports return ids and numbers only (no names, notes or free text); a report run and an export are audited, and a report whose audit entry cannot be saved is not delivered. Tests: `OperationalReportsApiTests`, `FinancialReportsApiTests`, `ClinicalReportsApiTests`, `MeasurementApiTests` (anonymous 401, wrong role 403, the right roles 200, operational and financial separate), `ReportExportApiTests` (a theory over all 12 export routes: an export gets exactly the answer the report gets for each role; a refused export leaves no audit entry; a run and an export are two audit entries), the existing permission-matrix and registry tests (in the focused run). Walkthrough ACCESS: billing refused the schedule report on the page and by the server (403), dentist refused production, front desk refused measurement, and none of those refusals added an audit row; the CSV and the audit entries contain none of the five patient names. Negative controls 1, 2 and 3 are caught.
