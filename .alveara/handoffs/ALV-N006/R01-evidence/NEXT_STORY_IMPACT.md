# ALV-N006 R01 - Next-story impact

Item 53 is the last story before **Gate E (Operations, Documents, and Data Ready)**, which has not started; no later story is started.

- **Gate E evidence.** The reports, the measurement panel, the CSV export, the metric definitions with versions, the immutable baselines and `docs/REPORTING.md` are the item 53 contribution. The frozen Stage 2 (`stage2/`) is the regression the gate can reuse; its counts are 5,612 backend tests, Vitest 1,879 in 120 files, mocked Playwright 138 and twelve walkthroughs.
- **Interfaces others may rely on.** `GET /api/reports/...` (twelve reports and their `/export`), the drill-down routes, `GET` and `POST /api/reports/measurement/baselines` (CSRF and `Idempotency-Key`), the `ReportResult` envelope (`reportId`, `definitionVersion`, `filter`, `generatedAtUtc`, `readConsistency`, `recordCount`, `noData`, `data`), the five new permissions (all grantable) and the audit events `ReportViewed`, `ReportExported` and `ReportBaselineSet`.
- **Rules a later change must keep.** A metric's definition version must be raised whenever what it counts changes (the pinned values in `MetricsTests` fail otherwise); a comparison is shown only when the baseline period ended before this one, the version is the same and both samples are at least 30; reports read only and are audited, and a report whose audit entry cannot be saved is not delivered; the CSV is made from the same report object as the screen.
- **Migration.** `20261029043602_AddReportBaselines` adds `ReportBaselines` and `ReportBaselineMetrics` and their triggers 51270 to 51274; any later migration must keep them (the rollback guard 51274 is untested).
- **Print.** Other pages print by hiding everything but their own region; the reports now do the same. A new printable page must do the same or print blank.
- **Known debt that touches the gate.** The billing-ledger walkthrough fails on `6899818` already (a selector that now matches three status paragraphs); the gate should fix or retire it before listing it as evidence. The comparison has no real-browser demonstration of a comparable change. Time spent on duplicate entry and on administrative work is not measurable by the application and is shown as such; the 20 to 30 percent overhead reduction is a target only.
