## Scope delivered (implementation scope of the prompt, item by item)
- **Safety alert model linked to patient and source/provenance** — `SafetyAlert` (category, title, detail, severity, status, **required `SourceNote`**, optional `SourceItemId` link to a clinical-record item), `Revision`, `RowVersion`; append-only `SafetyAlertVersion`.
- **Allergies, current medications, significant conditions, pregnancy, anticoagulant status, adverse reactions, unresolved clearance, authorized custom alerts** — allergies and active medications are read live from the clinical record (never copied); conditions, pregnancy, anticoagulant, adverse reaction and custom alerts are `SafetyAlert` categories a clinician states; clearances are their own workflow; all appear in one `SafetyContext`.
- **Severity/priority and active/resolved state** — Critical/High/Moderate/Low; Active/Resolved; allergy severity maps Severe→High, Moderate, Mild→Low, and an unrecorded severity stays unrecorded (flagged, never guessed).
- **Acknowledgement never equals resolution** — `SafetyAlertAcknowledgement`: per person and per revision, append-only, in its own table; the alert's status is untouched; resolving needs a reason and records who/when.
- **Clearance tracking with requested/received/resolved, actor/time/reason, supporting document later** — `Clearance`/`ClearanceVersion`; Received is not Resolved; a waiting clearance cannot be resolved; the document reference is optional and attachable later.
- **Patient-header / clinical-context projection for downstream screens** — `ISafetyContextProvider` (`GetAsync`, `SummaryAsync`) and the API; the header strip and the encounter use it.
- **Minimal board indicator** — `VisitCard.safety` = `{ alert, clearance }` for callers holding the new `ViewSafetyIndicator`.
- **Do not infer or invent** — an empty chart yields no entries and explicit gaps; no alert is created except by a clinician; category-level inference is never done.
- **UI** — persistent safety strip (patient header on every patient screen, and top of an open encounter), Safety tab with detail panel (source, status, last update), clearance workflow, acknowledged-versus-resolved wording, minimal board indicator.
- **Security/audit/integrity** — clinical permissions (server-side); resolution/reopen/cancel reasons required; no silent dismissal or deletion (triggers); audit of every change with user and time and generic, PHI-free text.
- **Failure paths** — conflicting/stale data (row version, revision, `alert_changed`, and `needsAttention` when an alert's source item was resolved or removed); alert source missing (`source_required`); resolve without a reason (`reason_required`); permission denied (403); supporting document not yet available (`documentPending`, attach later).

## Decisions and deviations from the prompt's wording
1. **Allergies and medications are read, not copied.** One source of truth: the clinical record. They appear in the safety picture with provenance and are changed there.
2. **"Authorized roles" for the board indicator = the clinical team** (Dentist, Hygienist, Assistant, Admin). Front desk and the practice manager still run the board but receive no safety information. This is the conservative reading; widening it is a one-line matrix change.
3. **A severe allergy raises the board's alert flag**, without naming it, because it is the single most safety-critical fact in the record; the flag does not say which source raised it.
4. **Alert categories exclude allergies, medications and clearances** (they have their own sources/workflow); the prompt's "relevant pregnancy flags, anticoagulant status, significant conditions, adverse reactions, custom" are the stated categories.
5. **Acknowledge requires the revision seen** (and is refused if it changed), so an acknowledgement can never cover something the person did not see.
6. **Audit text carries no category at all.** The audit log is readable by the practice manager, who cannot read clinical data, and a category such as "Pregnancy" is itself sensitive (found by the real-browser walkthrough's content check).
7. **Resolving a clearance without a document is allowed but flagged** ("document not yet attached") instead of refused, as the prompt's failure path implies; the reference is free text until a documents story exists.
8. **Writes on an inactive patient are allowed** for safety data (an inactive patient's allergy must stay correctable), unlike encounter documentation.

## Defects found and fixed by this attempt's own verification
- **Audit text disclosed the alert category** to a role that cannot read clinical data (found when the walkthrough's content check failed on "Anticoagulant"); fixed and tested.
- **A medication showed "Severity not recorded"** (found only by looking at the screenshot); the chip now appears only for allergies, with a test.
- **Two landmarks with the same name** (the header strip and the encounter's) failed the accessibility scan; the encounter's strip has its own accessible name.
- **Test-side races** (two) in the real-browser walkthrough: a text match that also matched an open textarea; fixed by waiting on the save indicator. Not product defects.
- **Mutation checks**: removing the board permission check and removing the "clearance must be received" rule each failed the intended tests.

## Limitations / debt
