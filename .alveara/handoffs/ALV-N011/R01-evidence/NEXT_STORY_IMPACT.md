# Next-story impact — ALV-N011 R01

Next scheduled item: **item 21 — `STORY-006` (original course story: interactive odontogram)**, executed only through its current course-portal prompt and unlock state; then item 22 `ALV-006-C01`.

- **Downstream consumers of the safety context** (odontogram, perio, diagnosis, treatment planning, procedure completion, prescriptions): inject `ISafetyContextProvider` (`GetAsync(patientId, userId)` for the full context, `SummaryAsync` for the counts) rather than reading the clinical record or alerts directly; or call `GET api/patients/{id}/safety[/summary]`. The patient header already shows the strip on every patient screen, so a screen that only needs a persistent reminder does not have to add one; an odontogram that wants safety information beside a tooth chart can reuse `SafetyStrip` (give it its own `label`, because landmark names must be unique on a page).
- **Reading rules downstream must keep:** show `entries` (active, most urgent first) and `gaps` together - an empty `entries` is **not** "no risk"; show `needsAttention`/`attentionReason` entries as needing review; never present "acknowledged" as "resolved"; do not infer a risk from anything not in the context. The context carries no drug-interaction or dose logic and none should be derived from it without a validated source (out of scope by design).
- **Prescriptions (a later story)** should treat `Allergy` and `Medication` entries (read from the clinical record) and active alerts as inputs, and must not ask the user to resolve an alert at the prescription step.
- **Interfaces added:** `SafetyContextService` / `ISafetyContextProvider`, `SafetyAlertService`, `ClearanceService`; `VisitBoardService.BoardAsync(..., includeSafety)`; permission `ViewSafetyIndicator` (appended last, after `ManageClinicalTemplates`); trigger numbers 51050-51054 are used, 51055 and up are free.
- **Migrations:** `AddPatientSafety` (this attempt); depends on `AddClinicalRecordAndNotes` (for the optional `SourceItemId` foreign key).
- **UI extension points:** `pages/safety/SafetyStrip.tsx`, the `safety` workspace tab (`patientWorkspaceTabs.ts`), the board card's `safety` field.
- **Documents:** the clearance's supporting document is a free-text `DocumentReference`. When a documents story exists, replace it with a real document link (a migration and an `AttachDocumentAsync` change), keeping the "document not yet attached" state.
- **Unresolved decisions that touch later work:** (1) whether front desk should see "Clearance open" on the board; (2) whether severe alerts need a second person to resolve them; (3) a tablet-width check of the clinical and safety screens (carried forward from the ALV-005-C01 review) before any tablet-first clinical use.
- **Prompt changes:** none required. `STORY-006` is a portal prompt; if its screens show clinical data they should respect `ViewClinicalDocumentation` and keep the safety strip visible (the header already does).
