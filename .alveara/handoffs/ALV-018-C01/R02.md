## Decisions and deviations
1. **Marker counted by the fixture, not by rows**, as the reviewer preferred.
2. **Tolerant minimum, exact total.** The check requires exactly four markers and at least one session each, because SQL Server may report extra sessions (for example a pooled connection of the test's own); the total of session lines must equal what the markers report.
3. **No change to the retry behaviour.** Attempts, delays, the first-exception rethrow and the diagnostic's safety rules are as before.
4. R01's decisions 1 to 9 stand (`R01.md`).
