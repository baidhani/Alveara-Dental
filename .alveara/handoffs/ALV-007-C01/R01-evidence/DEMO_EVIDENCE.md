## Truthful states shown
- Loading, empty and failed states: the unit tests show "Nothing has been billed to this patient yet" and an error state that says it could not load rather than a zero balance (the panel also draws a loading state, "Loading the billing account…", which no test asserts).
- Permission states: a reader sees everything and no control; a hygienist has no tab and a 403 from the API.
- Disconnected states: a dropped connection says "Not recorded: the connection dropped" and keeps what was typed (unit test).
- The secondary panels (responsibility, guarantor summary, payment methods) are left out, without any alert or guess, when they cannot be read.

The viewed screenshots (04 and 08) match what the tests assert. The sticky status bar overlays the lower part of the page in full-page captures; this is existing shell behaviour, not part of this story.
