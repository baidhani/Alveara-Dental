# Next-story impact - ALV-012-C01 R01

Next scheduled item: **item 25 - `STORY-013` (original course story: structured diagnosis linked to patient, encounter and treatment plan)**, executed only through its current course-portal prompt and unlock state. Then item 26, `ALV-013-C01` (its engineering companion: structured diagnosis lifecycle, coding provenance, context linkage and amendments), which is the one most likely to link a diagnosis to a periodontal chart.

- **Linking a diagnosis to a periodontal chart:** `PerioExamLinks` already holds links from a finalized chart to a `Diagnosis`, `TreatmentPlan`, `Encounter` or `HistoryEntry` by plain reference. A diagnosis story should create links through `PerioSessionService.LinkAsync(examId, linkType, reference, actor, ct)` (idempotent, audited, append-only) rather than writing the table, and can read a chart's links from `PerioExamView.Links`. The reference is opaque text today; nothing checks that the record exists, so the diagnosis story decides what a reference means.
- **Reading the chart:** `PerioSessionService.ExamAsync(examId)` and `GET /api/periodontal/charts/{id}` return a chart with readings, derived attachment loss, tooth records (mobility, furcation, not charted) and links; `PerioComparisonService` gives previous-versus-current figures a diagnosis or treatment plan can cite (the 2 mm trend rule and the cue thresholds are named constants in `PerioCues`).
- **Interfaces added:** `PerioSessionService`, `PerioComparisonService`, the pure `PerioSiteModel`, `PerioChartValidator`, `PerioComparison`; `PerioException` codes `session_closed`, `session_not_found`, `exam_not_found`, `row_version_required`, `row_version_invalid`, `no_previous_chart`; TypeScript `perioApi.ts` session and comparison clients and `perioSiteModel.ts`. The earlier STORY-012 interfaces are unchanged (optional fields were appended).
- **Triggers and migrations:** trigger numbers 51064 to 51070 are used by periodontal tables; **51071 and up are free**. Migrations `AddPeriodontalCharting` (STORY-012) and `AddPerioSessionsAndMeasures` (this attempt).
- **The missing-tooth rule is read, not enforced, here:** a session refuses readings on a tooth the odontogram records as missing at save time only; the odontogram's own invariant (`TR_ToothFindings_ToothPresence`) is unchanged. A story that writes into `ToothFindings` is still subject to that trigger.
- **UI extension points:** `PerioChartLinks` (where a diagnosis link could be offered from the diagnosis side), `PerioComparisonPanel`, the `Entry method` switch on the Periodontal tab.
- **Unresolved decisions that touch later work:** (1) furcation per tooth versus per furcation site; (2) whether the STORY-012 grid save should also read the odontogram; (3) the 2 mm trend threshold and whether the trend should also use attachment loss; (4) which story owns the numbering preference; (5) whether a diagnosis should be able to name the chart it came from.
- **Prompt changes:** none required for `STORY-013` (a portal prompt).
