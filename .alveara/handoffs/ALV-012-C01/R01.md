## Scope delivered (the prompt's implementation scope)
- **Explicit six-site per-tooth model** - `DB`, `B`, `MB`, `DL`, `L`, `ML` are the stored identity; how a site reads depends on the tooth ("mesial palatal" on an upper tooth, "mesial lingual" on a lower one, "mid facial" on a front tooth); all six read differently on all 32 teeth, in C# and TypeScript.
- **Probing depth, recession, bleeding** - as STORY-012, unchanged.
- **CAL by one documented rule** - probing depth plus recession at the same site, derived and never stored (so it cannot disagree with its parts); a tested function and a test that no field can store it.
- **Suppuration and plaque** - per site, true, false or not assessed (null), recorded only when ticked for the visit; **mobility** (Miller 0 to 3) and **furcation** (0 to 3, multi-rooted teeth only: molars and first upper premolars) per tooth.
- **Missing and excluded teeth without corrupting entry order** - a tooth can be marked not charted; it carries no readings or grades and is skipped by the entry order; a tooth the odontogram records missing is skipped, listed and refused (`tooth_absent`) with the way out named.
- **Periodontal diagnosis/history linkage** - a finalized chart can be linked, by reference, to a diagnosis, treatment plan, encounter or history entry (append-only, quiet repeats, who and when shown).
- **Finalized chart-session history and longitudinal comparison** - a chart is entered as a draft session and finalized into the immutable chart; two charts (or the draft and the last chart) are compared with whole-chart figures then and now and site by site.
- **UI** - a "Step by step (keyboard)" entry beside the existing grid (the grid stays the default); current tooth and site in large type with "site n of 192"; digits, Enter and quick keys (B, S, P, X, Shift+Enter) without leaving the depth box; a tooth strip with each tooth's state in words; cue marks in words with a line saying they are not a diagnosis; a comparison panel and trend sentence; the safety strip stays in the patient header.
- **Security, audit, integrity** - same permissions as STORY-012 (ViewClinicalDocumentation to read, ManageClinicalNotes to write, CSRF on every write); start, each save, finalize, abandon and link write a PHI-free audit entry in the same save; charts, tooth records, closed sessions and their entries are immutable by trigger.

## Decisions and deviations from the prompt's wording
1. **CAL is derived, not stored** - the prompt allows "derived/stored according to one documented rule"; derivation cannot drift from its parts and is the simplest rule to test.
2. **The immutable chart stays the finalized form; the draft is a separate editable record** - STORY-012's save, history and tests are untouched, and a finalized session becomes an ordinary chart.
3. **New measures are tri-state** - a measure not taken is not recorded as none; on screen they are recorded only when ticked for the visit.
4. **A refusal never changes the draft** - a batch is judged whole, so valid entries are neither lost nor half-applied; the screen keeps a buffer of what was typed.
5. **One documented entry order**, a snake along each arch, held identical in C# and TypeScript by a SHA-256 of the whole sweep asserted in both languages.
6. **Only the teeth a save touches are judged** - an unrelated odontogram change cannot block entry; the final save re-checks everything.
7. **Better or worse at 2 mm**, a plain stated threshold; the trend is by probing depth with attachment-loss change shown beside it.
8. **The STORY-012 grid save was left as it was** (it does not read the odontogram), rather than changing a verified contract; step-by-step entry does.
9. **A draft is discarded by being abandoned, never deleted**, so what was entered stays in the record.
10. **Furcation is one grade per tooth** (not per furcation site) - the prompt names furcation without site detail; per-site grading is a widening of one column and one rule.

## Limitations / debt
See `docs/PERIODONTAL.md` ("Limits (ALV-012-C01)"): furcation is one grade per tooth; permanent teeth only and no negative recession; one open draft per patient and no merge of two people's entries; what is typed since the last save is lost if the page is closed (at most the current tooth's sites, with a warning); comparison is by probing depth at 2 mm with no staging, grading or risk score; one fixed entry order; links are plain references that nothing checks; teeth recorded missing are read at save time only; and the STORY-012 grid save does not read the odontogram.
