## Why 9.0.21 is the narrowest compatible remediation
It is the first version both advisories name as fixed for the 9.0 line the graph already used (9.0.18 to 9.0.20 were affected); it satisfies `Microsoft.Data.SqlClient` 7.1.0's minimum (`>= 9.0.18`); it needs no change to `Microsoft.Data.SqlClient` (7.1.0) or `Microsoft.EntityFrameworkCore.SqlServer` (10.0.12). Newer versions exist (9.0.22, 9.0.23, and the 10.0.13+ line); none was chosen, to keep the change minimal and inside one major line. A direct reference wins over the transitive one, so the graph resolves 9.0.21.
