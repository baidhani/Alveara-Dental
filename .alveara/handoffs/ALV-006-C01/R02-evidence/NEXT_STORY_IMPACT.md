# Next-story impact - ALV-006-C01 R02

Next scheduled item: **item 23 - `STORY-012` (original course story: periodontal charting with probing depth, recession and bleeding)**, executed only through its current course-portal prompt and unlock state. Then item 24 `ALV-012-C01`.

- **Reuse for periodontal charting:** the tooth identity (`ToothKeys`, the 52 FDI strings; `ToothNumbering` for Universal, FDI and Palmer, permanent and primary) is the one identity every chart should use; periodontal sites belong to a tooth key, never a display number. On the client, `pages/odontogram/dentition.ts`, `ToothChart` (with its arrow-key navigation) and `toothNumbering.ts` are shared building blocks; the safety strip is already in the patient header.
- **The missing-tooth invariant is a database rule, and a perio story must respect it.** For every patient and tooth there is never an active `Absent`-effect finding in Existing or Completed beside an active finding with effect `None`. **If a later story writes into `ToothFindings` (a diagnosis or treatment story creating findings, or a procedure completing one), it is subject to the trigger `TR_ToothFindings_ToothPresence`: its write can be refused with SQL error 51061/51062 (or 51063 if the tooth's lock is busy) and should translate it as `OdontogramService` does (`tooth_absent`, `tooth_has_findings`, `tooth_busy`).** Anything that reads absence should read `ConditionType.ToothEffect`, never the word "Missing".
- **Reading rules downstream must keep:** an empty chart means "nothing recorded", never "healthy"; every state is a word as well as a style; findings on teeth a view does not draw must be listed, never hidden.
- **Interfaces added in R02:** `ToothPresenceErrors` (51061-51063) and the two refusal codes `tooth_has_findings` and `tooth_busy`. Earlier interfaces (`ConditionTypeService`, `OdontogramService.ToothHistoryAsync` and `LinkAsync`, `ConditionType`/`ConditionTypeEvent`/`ToothFindingLink`) are unchanged. Trigger numbers 51055-51063 are used; 51064 and up are free. No new permission.
- **Migrations:** `AddToothPresenceInvariant` (this attempt) after `AddConditionCatalogueAndLinks` and `AddOdontogram`; it adds a trigger only.
- **Diagnosis, treatment-plan and procedure stories** should call `OdontogramService.LinkAsync(findingId, "Diagnosis"|"TreatmentPlan"|"Procedure", reference, actor)`; linking never changes the finding. Moving a finding to Completed when its procedure completes is theirs to decide, and must expect the tooth-presence refusals above.
- **UI extension points:** `ConditionTypesPanel`, `ToothTimeline`, `FindingRow`'s link list, the `odontogram` workspace tab.
- **Unresolved decisions that touch later work:** (1) which story owns the numbering preference; (2) whether `ManageClinicalTemplates` is right for the catalogue; (3) whether an extraction should be a one-step action that retires the findings it makes moot; (4) whether linking should be automatic from the later stories; (5) carried forward: tablet-width validation of the clinical and safety screens (the odontogram has it).
- **Prompt changes:** none required for `STORY-012` (a portal prompt). The master plan's item 22 numbering bullet is already delivered; Palmer and the preference setting are in no item.
