Settled default-state scans confirm the reviewer's finding that the original `/showcase` danger-button hit is not a persistent default-state defect on the live page. The state-aware scans added while repairing `ALV-001-C01`, however, reproduced two real defects in this story's files:
1. `.alv-button--secondary:hover/focus` used `--color-primary` for its text. In the dark theme `--color-primary` is a button **background** (#1f4459), so hovered or focused secondary buttons were about **1.8:1** on the dark surface (axe `color-contrast`, serious) on every page that has one (reproduced on `/admin/users/:id`).
2. Computing the danger button's default text (`--color-danger` on its tinted background composited over the surface) gave **4.15:1** over `--color-surface` and **3.78:1** over `--color-surface-raised` in the dark theme (AA needs 4.5:1) - the latent cause of the original flagged danger-button hits.
