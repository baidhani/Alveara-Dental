# Next-story impact — ALV-005-C01 R01

Next scheduled item: `ALV-N011` — Patient Safety Alerts, Medical Risk Context, and Clearance Tracking (dependencies `ALV-005-C01`, `ALV-002-C01`, `ALV-011-C01`).

- **Data to build on:** the patient's allergies and medications are now a live, status-bearing list: `ClinicalRecordItem` (`Kind` = `Allergy` / `Medication`, `Status` `Active`/`Inactive`/`Resolved`/`Discontinued`, `Severity` for allergies) with full version history. An alert should read **Active** items only and treat `Resolved`/`Inactive`/`Discontinued` as history. `Severity` can be null (unknown), and a section can be `NoneKnown`, `Unknown` or `NotReviewed` — an alert must not show "no allergies" for `Unknown` or `NotReviewed`; those are different statements and are exposed by `ClinicalRecordReader` as the derived section `Status`.
- **Interfaces:** `GET api/patients/{id}/clinical-record` (sections, items, statuses, who reviewed and when); `ClinicalRecordReader` / `ClinicalRecordService` for server-side use. Reading needs `ViewClinicalDocumentation`; front desk and billing do not hold it — an alert shown on the schedule or board needs a deliberate decision about who may see a clinical flag (a cue without the detail, for example).
- **Extension points:** `ClinicalRecordItem` has no "alert" or "clearance" fields; `ALV-N011` should add its own tables keyed by patient (and optionally item id) rather than widening these. `ClinicalRecordEvent` is a PHI-free timeline it may link to. Item `RowVersion` and the append-only versions are the concurrency and history model to follow.
- **Migrations:** `AddClinicalRecordAndNotes` (this attempt). Trigger numbers 51040-51048 are used; 51049 and up are free.
- **UI extension points:** the clinical record panel (`pages/clinical/record/`) is where a per-patient alert summary could sit; the patient header (`PatientHeader`) is the shared place for a persistent cue. No alert banner exists yet.
- **Permissions:** `ManageClinicalTemplates` appended last; `ViewClinicalDocumentation` / `ManageClinicalNotes` unchanged.
- **Unresolved decisions that touch the next story:** (1) who may see a safety flag without clinical read access; (2) whether a severe allergy should appear on the encounter screen automatically (it does not today — the encounter and the record are separate stores); (3) how medical-risk context relates to `ClinicalRecordItem` kinds (this attempt added none).
- **Prompt changes:** none required. `ALV-N007` (AI drafting) can draft into the note sections `EncounterNote` provides (draft and unsigned only).
