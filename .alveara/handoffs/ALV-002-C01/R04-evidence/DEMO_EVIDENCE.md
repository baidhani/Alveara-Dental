# ALV-002-C01 R04 - Demo Evidence

Open **Audit Log** as an administrator at a narrow window: press Tab until the table area shows a clear blue focus outline, then use the Left/Right arrow keys to scroll the wide table; a screen reader announces "Audit log entries (scrollable table), region". The same works in the dark theme. A role without the permission still sees only the permission-denied message. Evidence: `artifacts/audit-keyboard-results.json` and `AuditLogPage.keyboard.test.tsx`.
