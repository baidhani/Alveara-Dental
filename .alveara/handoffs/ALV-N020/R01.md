## Decisions and deviations
1. **First-visit deep link (deliberate, per the guardrail):** a deep link opened before any sign-in no longer returns there after sign-in; the person lands on `/`. Two ALV-N009 route-guard tests were updated to expect the dashboard.
2. **13.05 is browser-level:** the outage is simulated by answering every `/api/**` request with 502 inside the browser (`page.route`), then removing it. The brief planned a runner-level stop and restart of the chain API; that runner step was not built.
3. **Alert source key** is `StaffInactivationFailed:<record id>`: `CK_Notifications_SourceKey` lists `-` in its character class but refuses it in practice (existing keys never used one). Not changed (out of scope).
4. **Record-before-notify** follows the migration decision exactly: no correlation-id fallback alert.
5. **The acting administrator** is among the alert recipients when they hold Admin or OfficeManager (decision text).
