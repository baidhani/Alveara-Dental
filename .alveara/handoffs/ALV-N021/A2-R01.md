## What changed
1. **One visit path, one policy (OD-01 to OD-12).** `Scheduled -> Confirmed -> Checked in -> Ready -> Seated -> In treatment -> Ready for checkout -> Completed`, with Confirm and Mark ready as practice settings (default required; changed on the Practice tab by `ManagePracticeConfiguration` holders, audited with old and new values, applied from the next move). STORY-011's shortcuts are refused on every route, legacy routes included. `VisitMovePolicy` decides who: front office, chairside, the booked provider for treatment, and completion by the front desk or by the Dentist/Hygienist who started treatment (new `CompleteOwnStartedVisit`, grantable only to Dentist and Hygienist). Check-in only on the appointment's practice day, lateness logged. Undo check-in with a reason before seating.
2. **One list of actions for both screens.** `GET api/appointments/{id}/actions` gives each signed-in user the moves they may make, blocked ones with the server's reason and the next-actor text; the Visit board and the calendar drawer draw only from it (proven equal in the browser, PARITY).
3. **Checkout-readiness gate (OD-22 to OD-32, migration M3).** Treatment ended, Patient departed, Follow-up and (when applicable) Financial must each have an outcome before completion; outcomes are append-only with corrections superseding; payment/waiver outcomes are verified against real ledger rows and the checkout never writes to the ledger. New `CollectPayments` (FrontDesk): take and apply payments, nothing else. One checkout panel on the board and in the calendar drawer.
4. **Scheduling integrity.** Provider/room reassignment through the booking guards with a reason and two Urgent notifications (migration M2; "Seen by" retired); blocked time over bookings needs confirmation and a reason, is refused over a visit under way, flags affected appointments until resolved, and check-in is refused while flagged; cancel needs a category (migration M1) with derived short notice; no-show is final with Rebook and Open patient.
5. **History and screens (Group C).** Each history row names who did it and, for moves, from and to (migration M4); labelled blocked time on the calendar through a ViewSchedule route that never returns the private reason (migration M5); week view shading, headers, narrow blocks, empty states; Schedule page status and links, "Appointment booked.", one refusal format; duration from the appointment type; a typing hint on time fields; the Component Showcase only in development builds.
6. **Time hardening (decision 02).** T-2 explicit invariant culture at every fixed-format date/time; T-3 one practice-day boundary rule on the clock.
7. **Merge guard repair (migration M6).** The final evidence run found that M1 added `Appointments.CancelCategory` without re-creating the patient-merge guard trigger, so a merge-time change of that column was not refused; M6 re-creates the trigger with the column.

Six additive migrations M1-M6, each with constraint/trigger tests, a rollback test and its inventory entry (real-migration inventory 28). New permissions `CompleteOwnStartedVisit`, `ReassignAppointmentProvider`, `CollectPayments` (appended; Permission Matrix, policy-health and delegation rules updated). New routes: actions, checkout read/record, undo check-in, reassignment, keep-despite-block, calendar blocked time, visit-workflow settings, and the test-only chain clock-advance route.
