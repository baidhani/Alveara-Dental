## Disclosures
1. **Client build defect, my own, now fixed.** From `dd57ee3` (slice 4) to `3dcaf8d` the client did not build: `DemographicReviewPage` imported `ApiError` from a module that does not export it.
   - Walkthrough B found it (the app could not load).
   - **My earlier client type checks were vacuous.** I ran `npx tsc --noEmit -p .` against the root `tsconfig.json`, which contains no files. Every "TypeScript passes" in my slice 1-6 reports was therefore unproven. The real checks (`-p tsconfig.app.json`, `-p tsconfig.node.json`) now pass, and are the ones in this package.
