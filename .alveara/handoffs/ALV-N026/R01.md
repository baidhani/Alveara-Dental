## Disclosures
1. **Allergy statement: dropped in S6, found by the package sweep, restored on owner decision (2026-11-22).**
   - The p2 PDF made in S6 printed no allergy-check outcome, which the old browser print had stated.
   - Deleting that print in S8 made the ALV-N022 wording guard (`tests/allergy-wording-guard.test.mjs`) fail. I had not run `node --test` during S6-S8.
   - The fix captures the outcome recorded when the prescription was written into the issue envelope, and prints the established wording ("a name comparison, not a clearance"). The matching allergy name is shown only for a recorded match, with no severity, reaction or note. The guard now checks the p2 template.
   - It is a narrowly scoped restoration of existing prescription information, not a new clinical decision feature.
   - An issue made before the restoration takes the outcome from the prescription's own recorded value.
   - The p2 pin changed from `6822dc58...` to `daa31289...`. That is a change to a reviewed template.
