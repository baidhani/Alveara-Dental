# ALV-015-C01 R01 - Next-story impact

Next scheduled item: **item 30 - `STORY-014`** (a course story, "Manage procedure/fee catalog with codes, descriptions, and fees"; its work was built earlier as `ALV-N005`). It is executed from the course portal's prompt, not from this attempt; nothing here blocks it. Later stories that touch this work: `STORY-007`/`ALV-007-C01` (appointments that a schedule state could link to), `STORY-016`/`ALV-016-C01` (prescriptions), the scheduling, completion and charge stories, and billing.

- **Assumptions to keep.** Acceptance is entered by staff on the patient's behalf; revisions are whole copies; a decision needs the plan's version while scheduling and completion do not; old references match only by reference code or id within the same patient and a tie is final.
- **Interfaces added.** `TreatmentPlanService` (partial: `.Revisions.cs`, `.States.cs`, `.Compare.cs`, `.ReferenceCode.cs`), `DiagnosisReconciliationService`, `RevisionLayout` and `ReviseInput`, `ItemAcceptance`, `ItemScheduling`, `ItemCompletion`, `ResolutionMethods`, the 11 endpoints (`docs/TREATMENT_PLAN_COMPLETION.md`); the plan and diagnosis views gained fields (additive).
- **Migration and triggers.** `AddTreatmentPlanCompletion`; error numbers 51099-51117 are used (13 triggers); **51118 and up are free**.
- **Interfaces a later story should use, not rebuild.** To tie an appointment to a procedure, add a nullable reference to a new state row or a new append-only table rather than a column on `TreatmentPlanItems` (procedures are immutable by trigger). To post a charge on completion, listen for the `ItemCompleted` history entry or read the latest `TreatmentPlanItemStates` row; the fee to charge is the item's copied `Fee` and `ProcedureVersionId`.
- **Procedure usage warning (a follow-up worth doing early).** The catalog's inactivation warning (`IProcedureUsageSource`) still counts odontogram links only. Plan procedures are not counted, so inactivating a procedure that plans use gives no warning (plans themselves are safe: fees are copied and an inactive procedure cannot be added). A small `IProcedureUsageSource` over `TreatmentPlanItems` (and, once they exist, charges) closes it.
- **UI extension points.** `TreatmentPlanItemStateLine` and `TreatmentPlanStateControls` (a new per-procedure state or action), `TreatmentPlanPhaseGroups` (a new grouping), `TreatmentPlanPatientCopy` (more on the patient page; keep it free of staff-only detail and of any insurance claim), `TreatmentPlanComparison` (a new kind of difference).
- **Diagnosis side.** `PlanReference` in `DiagnosisItem.tsx` shows a tied reference; a client can still never claim any state but `Unresolved`; `treatment_plan_reference_resolved` (and trigger 51116) lock the text once tied.
- **Patient-portal acceptance, signed acceptance and consent capture** are out of scope here; they would add a different "who entered it" (the patient) to the same state rows, and could link to a signed form of the forms module.
- **Test infrastructure.** The periodontal grid tests sit close to the 5 s default timeout (normally 1 to 3 s, 5 to 7 s under load); recommended: a larger timeout for them or for the suite, before the frontend suite grows further.
- **Unresolved decisions that touch later work.** Whether billing roles may read plan fees; whether a tie of an old reference should ever be undoable; whether the catalog should refuse to inactivate a procedure that open plans use or only warn.
- **Prompt changes.** None required for `STORY-014`. A future prompt for completion or charges should say whether a completed procedure's fee comes from the plan (copied) or from the catalog on the completion date.
