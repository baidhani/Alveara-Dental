## Failure paths named in the prompt
Missing diagnosis or procedure: STORY-015's per-field refusals, unchanged; a missing, malformed, duplicate or cross-patient diagnosis reference: the reconciliation tests (no match, ambiguous = duplicate target, cross-patient); referenced procedure inactive: STORY-015's rule and trigger 51097 (an inactive procedure cannot be added; a plan keeps its copied fee), unchanged; stale plan edit: the row-version tests above; partial acceptance conflict: item 5.
