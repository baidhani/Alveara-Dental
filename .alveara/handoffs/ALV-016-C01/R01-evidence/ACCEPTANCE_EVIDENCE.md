| 6 | **Prescription actions are audited and finalized safely** | Every action writes a PHI-free audit entry with the user, time and a correlation id in the same save: `PrescriptionDraftCreated`/`Changed`/`Discarded`/`SafetyAcknowledged`, `PrescriptionAmendmentStarted`, `PrescriptionRecorded`, `PrescriptionSuperseded`, `PrescriptionCancelled`, `PrescriptionDiscontinued`, `PrescriptionPrinted`; if the entry cannot be written nothing is saved (`When_the_log_cannot_be_written_...` for create, finalize, cancel and print). Permissions: seven roles over every route (`The_clinical_team_reads_the_workflow_and_everyone_else_is_refused`, `Only_dentists_and_administrators_can_change_anything_in_the_workflow`), a missing CSRF token on every change route, 401 for anonymous; walkthrough "READ-ONLY". Walkthrough "TRUST" checks the audit log: one `PrescriptionRecorded` per finalized prescription, each with the dentist's id, a time and a correlation id, and no medication, dose, allergy or reason anywhere in the workflow's audit entries; `10-failed-log-nothing-finalized.png`. |

## The prompt's failure paths
- **Known allergy or safety conflict requiring clinician review according to configured policy:** `A_medication_that_matches_a_recorded_allergy_is_not_finalized_until_the_prescriber_gives_their_own_words`, `An_allergy_list_that_was_never_reviewed_is_never_treated_as_clear_at_finalization`; walkthrough "ALLERGY MATCH" and "UNREVIEWED ALLERGIES" (`05-allergy-match-needs-review.png`, `06-unreviewed-allergies.png`).
- **Missing or structurally invalid prescription data:** criterion 3 above.
- **Stale allergy or medication list update:** criterion 4 above.
- **Print or render failure:** `A_prescription_without_instructions_quantity_and_refills_cannot_be_printed_and_a_failed_log_prints_nothing`, screen tests "says nothing was printed when the print cannot be prepared" and "... when the browser cannot open the print dialog"; F03 and F04 killed.
- **Unsupported request for clinical dose appropriateness when no authoritative source is configured:** criterion 5 above.
