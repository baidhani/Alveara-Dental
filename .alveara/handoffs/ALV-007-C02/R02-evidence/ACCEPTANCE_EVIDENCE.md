## 2. Refund/reversal preserves original transactions and a reconstructable balance
- **Gate D1 reconciliation:** `BillingReconciliationTests` - **70 real transactions** through the service on SQL Server (payments with and without named charges, applications, refunds and the releases they cause, moves, corrections of payments and of adjustments, adjustments, reversals of payments, of adjustments and of charges), kept against an independent model that knows only the plain meaning of each operation. After **every** transaction: the balance equals the model to the cent; balance = open charges - unapplied payments + net adjustments; each live payment's refunded + applied + unapplied equals what it was; each live charge's paid + open equals what it was; what payments applied equals what charges were paid; the preserved history verifies clean; statements read every 12 transactions agree with the account (balance, open total, unapplied, line balances, aging buckets sum to the open total); a statement issued mid-walk reprints byte for byte at the end; receipts agree with the payments' standing; the timeline holds every entry, application, release and the issued document. Coverage asserted: each of pay, refund, move, correct-payment, correct-adjustment, reverse-payment, reverse-adjustment and adjust at least twice, and at least three releases.
- Math without a database: `BillingRefundMathTests` (10), including cent drift over a thousand refunds and releases and every new `Verify` finding.
- Refund rules: `BillingRefundServiceTests` (25) - unapplied first, then newest applications (D2), cap at the payment less earlier refunds, a reason and an active method required, a reversed payment cannot be refunded, a refunded payment cannot be reversed and a refund cannot be reversed, no credit (the balance only goes up), conflict from an out-of-date account.
- The database on its own (a script, a future module or a person with database access): `BillingRefundSchemaTests` (60) - 51157 to 51171 each refused with its number, including that a charge whose applications are all released can be reversed and one partly paid cannot (51141), and that the two triggers guarding a refund's method each refuse alone (51143, 51161).
- Move and correction (D3, D4): the moves, the equal totals, the duplicate, zero, fractional, empty, over-release and no-op rows named beside their field, the replacement starting unapplied, charges reversal-only, the no-credit rule before and after the replacement.
- Atomicity: `BillingStatementsApiTests.When_the_log_cannot_be_written_a_refund_a_move_a_correction_and_an_issue_answer_503_and_store_nothing_not_even_the_releases_a_move_saved_first` (counts of entries, applications, releases and documents unchanged; the same request then succeeds).
- Walkthrough "REFUND", "MOVE", "RECEIPT, THEN CORRECTION" through the screen.

## 3. Duplicate submission cannot duplicate a refund/reversal
- `The_same_refund_again_changes_nothing_and_the_same_key_for_something_else_is_refused`, `The_same_move_again_changes_nothing_in_any_row_order_and_a_different_one_under_the_key_is_refused`, `A_repeat_of_a_correction_changes_nothing` (in `A_correction_cannot_leave_the_account_owing_less_than_nothing_and_a_repeat_changes_nothing`), `Issuing_the_same_preview_with_the_same_key_returns_the_same_document_and_another_use_of_the_key_is_refused`.
- **Deterministic concurrency controls** (`BillingRefundConcurrencyTests`, 5; a hooked clock lets a competing request finish between the first request's checks and its save, so the race happens every time): of two refunds that together exceed the payment only one is recorded; a double-submitted refund is recorded once and the retry returns the recorded one; of two moves of the same money only one is recorded (one release, one new application); of two corrections of one payment only one; two statements issued at once take numbers 1 and 2, never the same number twice. The loser gets the shared concurrency conflict and records nothing; the ledger verifies clean.
- UI: the key is kept while the request is unchanged (UI tests "keeps the same key for an unchanged retry"); walkthrough "STALE SECOND SCREEN".

## 5. Guarantor responsibility is reflected consistently
- `A_statement_is_addressed_to_who_was_responsible_on_the_day_and_a_reprint_keeps_that_addressee` - after responsibility moves to another patient the statement is addressed to that patient and shows the account patient as the subject; after responsibility moves back and the guarantor is renamed, the reprint still shows the addressee as resolved on the day (stored in the envelope) with the same hash, and a new preview shows the new addressee. A receipt is for the payer (`BillingDocumentBuilderTests`, receipt content).
- Refund recipient (D2): the money goes back to whoever the payment records as having paid it; the request has no recipient field, so there is no replacement recipient (`RefundRequest`; UI test "says what will happen ... ## Review finding ALV-007-C02-R01-01 (corrected in R02): the timeline's source link must work for a billing-only reader
The timeline is read with `ViewBilling`, and R01 rendered every completed-procedure source as a link to the clinical history, which Billing, FrontDesk and OfficeManager (billing without clinical-documentation access) cannot read. R02 gives the link an authorized destination and keeps the direct clinical link only for those who may use it.
- **Destination:** `GET api/patients/{id}/billing/entries/{n}/source` (`ViewBilling`), a narrow billing projection (`ChargeSourceView`): the procedure, the tooth and surface, the charge and whether it was reversed, whether a completion record exists, when it was recorded, the performed time and whether it was amended. It has no provider, encounter, chart effect, diagnosis or clinical wording. A procedure completed the old, state-only way is shown as having no completion record.
- **A billing-only role can follow the link and still cannot read the clinical record** (`BillingChargeSourceApiTests`, 11 tests, real API and SQL Server): `A_billing_only_reader_can_follow_a_charges_source_and_still_cannot_read_the_clinical_record` (the timeline names the completed procedure, the source opens with 200 and carries no field whose name mentions a provider, encounter, chart, diagnosis, note, rationale or finding and none of the private note text, while the same role gets 403 on `GET api/patients/{id}/completed-procedures` and on `GET api/completed-procedures/{id}`); `A_charge_completed_the_old_way_has_a_source_that_says_no_completion_record_exists`; `Whoever_can_see_billing_can_read_a_charges_source_and_nobody_else_can` (Admin, OfficeManager, Billing, Dentist, FrontDesk 200; Hygienist and Assistant 403; anonymous 401); `A_source_is_only_for_a_charge_of_that_patient_and_never_another_patients_or_another_kind_of_entry` (another patient's route and a payment are `source_not_found`); `A_reversed_charge_says_so_and_a_later_amendment_of_the_performed_time_is_shown_as_amended`.
- **UI** (`BillingStatementsPanel.test.tsx`, 4 new): the source opens from the timeline with no clinical permission and shows nothing clinical (a payment has no source button); the direct clinical link appears only with the clinical permission (and then the billing source is still offered); a charge completed the old way says so; a failed load says so. The walkthrough step "TIMELINE AND AGING" does the same in a real browser as the billing user (no clinical link, the source panel, API 200 for the source and 403 for the clinical record; axe light and dark clean; screenshot 16).
- **Reproduced before the fix:** the 11 API tests were written first and all failed (the destination did not exist); they pass now.
- **Negative controls:** N26 (the source route needs ManageBilling), N27 (not scoped to the patient in the route), N28 (answers for an entry that is not a charge with a source) and N29 (UI: the clinical link shown to anyone) are all killed.
