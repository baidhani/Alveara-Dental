# ALV-007-C02 R01 - Next-story impact

The next scheduled items are 36 `ALV-008-C02` (retire the legacy completion paths and adopt historical completions), then 37 `STORY-016`. Gate D (after item 38) criteria D1 (reconciliation) and D3 (statement, receipt, refund, reversal and account UX hardening) depend on this story. Nothing of item 36 was started.

## For ALV-008-C02 (retire legacy completion; adopt historical completions)
- **The timeline and statements already treat a charge with no completed-procedure record correctly.** A charge made for a procedure completed the old way (state only) has no `CompletedProcedures` row: its timeline entry has no `completedProcedureId` and no source line, and its aging date falls back to the time the charge was recorded. When `ALV-008-C02` adopts such a completion, the charge starts to age from the adopted performed time and its timeline entry gains the link, **without changing any issued document** (an issued document ages with the amendments effective at its own instant, and adoption is not an amendment; if adoption is built as an amendment of a performed time it is applied only to documents issued after it).
- **Issued documents are immutable and reproducible.** Anything `ALV-008-C02` writes that an issued statement reads (a completed procedure's performed time or its amendments) must be append-only with a precise `AmendedAtUtc` that is not before the real time it was recorded, because a reprint uses "amendments made at or before the document's instant". Backdating `AmendedAtUtc` would change what old documents show and make a reprint fail with `document_content_changed`.
- **No billing change is needed to retire the legacy routes**; the billing tests that complete an item the old way (`BillingTestBase` and its subclasses) are the callers already listed in the ALV-008-C01 handoff, and the new ledger tests (`BillingRefund*`, `BillingDocument*`, `BillingTimelineAgingTests`, `BillingReconciliationTests`) use `BillablePlanAsync` and `CompletionTestBase` helpers the same way, so they join that list: `BillingRefundServiceTests`, `BillingRefundConcurrencyTests`, `BillingDocumentServiceTests`, `BillingTimelineAgingTests`, `BillingReconciliationTests`, `BillingStatementsApiTests` (the last completes through the HTTP legacy route in its arrangement).
- **Gate D1 (reconciliation)** is straightforward from this story's data: `BillingReconciliationTests` is the model; a completed plan item with a fee above zero has exactly one charge, and `GET .../billing/verification` rebuilds the balance from the preserved history.

## For STORY-016 and later stories
- **Triggers 51172 and up are free** (billing owns 51118-51143 and 51157-51171, completion 51144-51156).
- Statements, receipts, the timeline and aging are derived and **never a balance source**; any later report (for example a practice-wide aging report or an insurance statement) should reuse `FinancialDocumentBuilder.AgingAt` and `BillingLedgerMath` rather than re-adding amounts.
- `FinancialDocumentBuilder.TemplateVersion` is 1. Changing what a statement or receipt contains means a new template version **and** keeping the old builder, because reprinting an old document rebuilds it with the code that issued it; the stored version is checked at issue and a reprint of a document with a version the code no longer has must fail safe (it currently reports `document_content_changed`).
- A consolidated guarantor statement, a statement for a period, PDF output, credit balances and refunds to someone other than the original payer were deliberately left out (decisions D2, D7, D9) and need their own design.

## Shared infrastructure and conventions
- `BillingService` is a partial class (`.cs`, `.Ledger`, `.Methods`, `.ProcedureCharge`, `.Queries`, `.Responsibility`, and now `.Refunds`, `.Corrections`, `.Documents`, `.Timeline`). Add a partial rather than grow a file; the biggest is `BillingService.Documents.cs` at about 280 lines.
- Every call to `BillingLedgerMath` that derives a number from allocations must pass the account's releases (`Ledger.MathReleases`); an omitted argument compiles and is wrong once a release exists. The repository search used to check this was `BillingLedgerMath\.(Unapplied|OpenCharges|Paid|Applied|Verify)\(` and no call is left without releases.
- Test helpers: `BillingRefundConcurrencyTests.RaceAsync` (a hooked clock lets a competing request finish between a request's checks and its save; it relies on the first `clock.UtcNow` call being after the checks, so a new write operation needs its clock read there too), `BillingReconciliationTests` (a seeded walk against an independent model), and the `DISABLE TRIGGER` pattern in `BillingRefundSchemaTests` for proving each of two guards of one rule on its own.
- Real-backend walkthrough config: `playwright.billing-statements.config.ts`; run it with the other walkthroughs in the sweep (`C:/tmp/run_regress_c02.sh` is the sweep script used here, not committed).

## UI extension points
`BillingPanel` composes the account screen; the new pieces are `BillingRefundForm`, `BillingMoveForm`, `BillingCorrectForm`, `BillingDocuments` (preview, issue, reprint, print, CSV, driven by `billingStatementsApi.ts`), `BillingDocumentView` (`StatementView`, `ReceiptView`) and `BillingTimelineAging`. The print style is in `Billing.css` (`@media print` shows only `.alv-bill__print`). The timeline's "Clinical history" link goes to `/patients/{id}/history`; a billing role cannot read clinical documentation, so for them the link leads to a page that refuses them (the timeline's own text still names the procedure).

## Unresolved decisions to carry
- Whether the timeline's source link should open the specific completed procedure once the clinical history can deep-link to one, and whether a billing-only role should get a read-only completed-procedure summary (as billing already gets a read-only billable-procedures list).
- Whether a refund should one day be allowed to a recipient other than the original payer (an explicit, audited exception), and whether credit balances are wanted.
- How a document should behave if the practice's name or address becomes part of the display envelope (today the envelope holds only the patient, the addressee and the currency).
