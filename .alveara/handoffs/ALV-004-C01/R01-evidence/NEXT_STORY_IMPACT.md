## What later stories can rely on
- **An appointment has a status and a history.** `Appointment.Status` is `Scheduled`, `Cancelled` or `NoShow`; **only `Scheduled` appointments hold a provider, operatory or patient**. STORY-011 adds *visit-lifecycle* states (checked in, in treatment, completed…): it must decide how they relate to `Status`. The safe approaches are (a) a separate flow-state column/table keyed by the appointment, leaving `Status` meaning "is this slot held", or (b) new `Status` values — in which case `SchedulingGuards` (`a.Status == Scheduled`) must be reviewed so a checked-in or completed appointment still holds (or deliberately stops holding) its time. Do not add a status without deciding that.
- **One conflict rule, one lock.** `SchedulingGuards.AcquireLocksAsync` (provider, operatory, patient, sorted, transaction-scoped) and `ThrowIfConflictAsync` are shared by booking and rescheduling. Anything that creates or moves an appointment (a recall booking, a walk-in, an online-booking story) must go through `AppointmentScheduler` / `AppointmentManager` — not insert rows — or the race guarantees are lost.
- **History is append-only** (`AppointmentEvent`: `Scheduled`, `Rescheduled` with the previous start/provider/operatory, `Cancelled` with the reason, `NoShow`, `NotesChanged`). A flow-state story can add its own event types; keep details PHI-free in the audit log and in the appointment's own tables only.
- **Concurrency**: every change to an appointment needs the `rowVersion` it read (`AppointmentView.RowVersion`); a stale one is the shared 409. `EnsureCurrent` in `AppointmentManager` is the pattern.
- **Reading**: `GET /api/appointments?...&includeAll=true` is the calendar's all-status list; without it you get what is still booked. `GET /api/appointments/{id}/history` for the events. A patient-workspace "Appointments" tab could reuse `Appointment`/`listCalendar` with a patient filter — there is **no patient filter on the list endpoint yet**.
- **Check-in readiness (STORY-011 / ALV-011-C01)** reads `Appointment` + the forms tables (ALV-N010: `PatientForms`/`SignedFormSnapshots`, `Status = 'Signed'`); there is still no required-forms list.
- **Frontend**: `calendarLayout.ts` (pure local-time arithmetic, lane placement, `practiceNow`), `CalendarGrid`, the drawer panels, `explainRefusal` (turns a refusal into plain words naming what is in the way). The calendar's blocks are `button`s with a complete accessible name; a new status needs a word in `statusWord` and a style in `Calendar.css` (written in the block, never colour alone).
- **Permissions**: unchanged — `ManageAppointments` to change, `ViewSchedule` to read.
- **Measurement**: `appointment.rescheduled`, `appointment.cancelled`, `appointment.no-show` (schema v1, `outcome` only) alongside `appointment.scheduled` / `appointment.rejected`.
