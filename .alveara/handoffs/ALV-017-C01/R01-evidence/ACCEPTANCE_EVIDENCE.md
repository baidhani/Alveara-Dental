## 2. No merge occurs without explicit human confirmation
- Server: `PatientMergeServiceTests.Without_explicit_confirmation_nothing_is_merged_however_complete_the_request_is` (confirmed false, and not sent); over HTTP `PatientMergeApiTests.A_merge_needs_explicit_confirmation_and_records_that_look_like_different_people_need_their_own`.
- A second, separate confirmation when the records look like different people (different date of birth or a name that is not a nickname): `PatientMergeServiceTests.Records_that_look_like_different_people_need_their_own_confirmation_and_a_genuine_duplicate_does_not`.
- No silent auto-merge: the scan only lists candidates (`PatientDuplicateScannerTests`); nothing in the code merges without the request above. The screen keeps Merge off until every field is answered, a reason is written and the merge is confirmed: `PatientMergeScreens.test.tsx` "shows the records side by side and keeps the merge button off until ...", and "warns when the records look like different people and keeps the merge off until that is confirmed on purpose". Walkthrough steps 2 and 3.
- Negative controls: B1 (confirmation skipped), B2 (identity confirmation skipped), U6 (screen skips it): all killed.

## 3. Representative linked clinical, financial, document and task data survives
- `PatientMergeChartPreservationTests.One_merge_carries_a_whole_real_chart_across_the_domains_to_the_survivor_with_nothing_lost_and_everything_still_agreeing`: a chart is built through the real services (an appointment, a medical-history item and an allergy, a tooth finding, a finalized periodontal session, a diagnosis, a plan with a completed procedure, a charge and a payment on the account, a prescription, a document, a referral, a follow-up task, a household and a person the patient is guarantor for); one merge moves every row of every registered column to the survivor, nothing is left or doubled, the money is the same to the cent and the account still takes a payment, the audit entries naming the retired record are untouched and still resolve through the lineage, and every foreign-key edge agrees.
- The registry that decides what a merge must move is checked against the database model: `PatientOwnedDataTests` (every patient-owned column of every table is registered or is bookkeeping; a new one fails the build).
- Rows the survivor already has one of (for example a unique insurance number) stay with the retired record, with everything that depends on them: `PatientMergeServiceTests.A_record_the_survivor_already_has_its_one_of_stays_with_the_retired_patient_and_so_does_everything_that_depends_on_it`; a record that cannot move blocks the merge with nothing changed: `A_merge_is_blocked_when_a_record_that_must_stay_points_at_one_that_would_move_and_nothing_is_changed`.
- A restored backup keeps the lineage, ledger and dismissals: `PatientMergeBackupRestoreTests` (2).

## 4. Conflicting values require resolution
- `PatientMergeServiceTests.A_field_conflict_needs_an_answer_and_each_answer_is_applied_and_recorded_with_the_value_it_replaced`; over HTTP `PatientMergeApiTests.A_record_changed_after_it_was_reviewed_is_409_and_a_conflicting_field_needs_an_answer_over_http`.
- The audit entry names the fields decided and how, never their values: `PatientMergeServiceTests.The_audit_text_names_the_fields_that_were_decided_and_how_but_never_their_values` (negative control B5).
- Conflicting identities need their own confirmation (criterion 2). Side-by-side comparison and one control per conflict: `PatientMergeScreens.test.tsx` and walkthrough steps 2 and 3 (`02-review-side-by-side.png`, `03-review-answered-and-confirmed.png`).

## 5. The merge is transactional or safely recoverable
- Transactional: `PatientMergeRecoveryTests.A_merge_that_fails_part_way_changes_nothing_and_the_same_request_succeeds_once_the_fault_is_gone` fails the merge at four points (the lineage, the rows moving, the retired record marked, the audit entries) and shows nothing changed and the same request then succeeds once; `PatientMergeServiceTests.When_the_audit_write_fails_nothing_is_merged_and_the_same_request_succeeds_once_it_works`.
- Already-merged and changed-record attempts: `PatientMergeRecoveryTests.A_second_attempt_at_a_record_that_is_already_merged_is_refused_and_changes_nothing_more`, `A_change_made_to_either_record_after_the_review_stops_the_merge_with_nothing_moved`; a repeat with the same key is the merge already made (`PatientMergeServiceTests.A_repeat_of_the_same_request_is_the_merge_already_made_...`).
- Recoverable: a merge keeps a ledger of every row it moved (`PatientUnmergeSchemaTests.A_merge_writes_a_ledger_of_exactly_the_rows_it_moved_...`); `PatientUnmergeServiceTests.A_merge_and_its_undo_leave_every_row_of_both_charts_where_it_was_and_the_record_active_again_with_its_own_lineage_and_audit`. The undo is previewed first by doing the work in a transaction that is rolled back (`The_preview_does_the_work_and_rolls_it_back_...`), refuses without confirmation, a reason or a current version, refuses when records made since depend on moved rows or a ledger row is no longer where it was (nothing changed), keeps a field edited after the merge, fails part way with nothing changed (four points), and can be repeated safely. Over HTTP and on the screen: `PatientMergeApiTests.A_merge_can_be_previewed_and_undone_over_http_...`, the undo screen tests, walkthrough steps 6 and 7.
- Where undo is not offered (stated, tested): a merge made before ledgers existed, and a merge whose survivor was itself merged away since (`A_merge_made_before_ledgers_existed_cannot_be_undone_and_neither_can_one_whose_survivor_was_merged_away_since`).

## 6. Lineage remains visible
- The retired record stays in place, merged and inactive, with lineage, history and audit saying who and when (`PatientMergeServiceTests.The_retired_patient_stays_in_place_merged_and_inactive_...`); lineage, moves and resolutions are never changed or deleted (`PatientMergeSchemaTests.The_lineage_its_moves_and_its_resolutions_are_never_changed_or_deleted`).
- The patient's own page reads where it stands: `PatientMergeApiTests.A_patients_own_page_can_read_where_it_stands_in_the_merge_lineage_whoever_may_view_patients`; the banner (`MergeHistoryBanner.test.tsx`, 5 tests); the retired record is read-only and offers no save or reactivate; a new record for a merged-away patient is refused by the database and answered as a clear 409 (`RetiredRecordGuardTests`, 6). Walkthrough `04b-retired-record-banner.png`, `04c-surviving-record-lineage.png`, `05-merge-log.png`.
