## 1. A referral can be created, tracked and completed with follow-up
- Created once per Idempotency-Key, with a party, a reason, a due date, notes: `ExternalCaseServiceTests.A_referral_is_a_draft_with_its_history_and_an_audit_entry_that_does_not_carry_the_reason`, `Making_the_same_referral_twice_with_the_same_key_gives_one_case_and_a_different_request_under_that_key_is_refused`, `Two_identical_creates_at_once_make_one_case`; through the API `ExternalCaseApiTests.A_referral_is_made_once_per_key_and_goes_through_its_statuses_with_a_history_that_names_who`.
- Tracked and completed: `ExternalCaseServiceTests.A_referral_goes_from_draft_to_sent_to_received_to_completed_with_its_dates_closure_and_history`, `A_cancellation_needs_its_reason_and_a_cancelled_case_is_final`, `A_move_the_rules_do_not_allow_an_unknown_status_and_a_bad_date_are_refused_and_store_nothing`; rules in `ExternalCaseRulesTests` (moves, notes, dates); the shape each status needs in the database: `ExternalCaseSchemaTests.A_referral_in_each_status_has_exactly_the_dates_and_closure_that_status_needs`.
- Follow-up: the chase task. `ExternalCaseTaskJobTests.A_sent_referral_that_reached_its_due_date_gets_one_unassigned_task_with_its_history_and_audit_entry`, `However_many_passes_and_runs_there_are_one_job_and_one_task_per_case_and_due_date`, `When_the_case_is_finished_the_open_task_is_closed_by_the_system_with_the_right_words` (completed and cancelled), `A_task_a_person_already_closed_is_left_alone_when_the_case_is_later_finished`.
- Concurrency and failure paths: `A_stale_version_is_refused_and_two_people_moving_the_same_case_at_once_cannot_both_win`, `An_edit_needs_the_current_version_changes_something_and_a_sent_case_keeps_its_party`, `ExternalCaseApiTests.A_stale_edit_and_two_people_moving_one_case_at_once_cannot_both_win_and_lose_nothing`, `ExternalCaseTaskJobTests.When_the_audit_entry_cannot_be_written_nothing_is_stored_and_the_retry_makes_the_one_task`, `A_job_interrupted_by_a_restart_is_recovered_without_making_a_second_task`; missing external party: `A_case_needs_an_active_outside_party_of_the_matching_kind_and_a_real_patient`.
- On screen against the real stack: walkthrough `external-cases-real-backend.spec.ts` (REFERRAL, CHASE, COMPLETE; screenshots `03-referral-detail.png`, `04-queue-chase.png`, `05-referral-completed.png`).

## 2. A lab case can link to a treatment and tooth and track status and dates
- Tooth, material, shade, treatment-plan item (its tooth is the default): `ExternalCaseServiceTests.A_lab_case_for_a_planned_treatment_takes_the_planned_tooth_unless_another_is_given`, `The_text_the_dates_the_tooth_the_encounter_and_the_plan_item_are_checked_before_anything_is_stored` (another patient's plan item and a bad tooth are refused), `ExternalCaseSchemaTests.A_referral_names_an_encounter_of_its_own_patient_and_a_lab_case_a_plan_item_of_its_own_patient`, `A_case_needs_a_plain_reason_or_title_and_plain_notes_within_their_limits`.
- Status and dates (including the lab-only InProgress and the remake loop, which clears the received date and needs a note): `ExternalCaseServiceTests.A_lab_case_goes_out_is_acknowledged_comes_back_and_may_be_sent_back_for_a_remake_which_needs_a_note`, `ExternalCaseSchemaTests.A_lab_case_may_also_be_in_progress_and_has_the_same_shapes`, `ExternalCaseApiTests.A_lab_case_links_a_tooth_goes_out_and_back_and_may_be_sent_back_for_a_remake_with_a_note`; overdue chase for a lab case: `ExternalCaseTaskJobTests.A_lab_case_that_reached_its_due_date_gets_a_lab_task_and_an_overdue_case_gets_a_high_priority_one`.
- On screen: walkthrough LAB (a tooth that is not two digits is refused on the form, the remake needs a note, a stale attempt after someone else's edit is explained and stores nothing, then the same move succeeds and clears the received date; `06-lab-stale.png`).
