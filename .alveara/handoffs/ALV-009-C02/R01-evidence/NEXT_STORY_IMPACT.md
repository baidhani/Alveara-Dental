# ALV-009-C02 R01 - Next-story impact

Next in the plan: item 48, STORY-017 (a course story executed in the course portal: duplicate detection and controlled merge of patient records), then item 49, ALV-017-C01 (safe duplicate review and merge preservation). **Not started.**

- **A merge must move these records.** ALV-017-C01 lists "referrals/tasks" among the data a merge preserves. This story adds `ReferralCases`, `LabCases` (both with `PatientId`), `ExternalCaseEvents` and `ExternalCaseDocuments` (reached through the case), and `FollowUpTasks.SourceReferralCaseId`/`SourceLabCaseId`. A database trigger refuses to change a case's patient (51221) and a link to a document of another patient (51222) and a chase task naming a case of another patient (51224); a merge will therefore need a deliberate, audited path (a controlled update by a merge service that moves cases, their documents and their chase tasks together, or an exemption in the triggers for that one service). That is a design decision for ALV-017-C01, not made here.
- **Reusable:** system tasks with a unique source key and job-driven creation and closure (`ExternalCaseTaskScheduler` and its handlers follow the recall pattern); the party list as a reference-data pattern (never deleted, made inactive); linking documents by id with broken-link flags; the derivation script for the identity manifest (`derive_identity_manifest.py`), which cross-checks the filters against `--list-tests`.
- **Permissions:** `ViewExternalCases`, `ManageExternalCases` and `ManageExternalParties` are not protected, so they can be granted, revoked and delegated through the ALV-N018 policy.
- **Not done and possibly wanted later:** electronic referral or lab exchange (out of scope); patient-facing notices; an operator reset for a chase job that fails all attempts (it stays `Failed`); a way to import a document from inside a case (documents are linked from the library); the treatment-plan screen showing a lab case beside its item.
- **Operational:** the chase pass runs inside the existing `FollowUps:WorkPollSeconds` loop (default 300, 0 = off).
