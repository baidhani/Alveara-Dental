## Scope delivered
- **Outside parties:** referral providers and lab vendors (name unique within kind, contact details), never deleted, made inactive; a case names an active party of the matching kind.
- **Referral cases:** reason, party, due ("follow up by") date, optional visit, notes; Draft, Sent, Received, Completed, Cancelled.
- **Lab cases:** what is being made, party, due date, tooth, treatment-plan item (its tooth is the default), material, shade, notes; the same statuses plus InProgress and a remake loop (Received back to Sent, which needs a note and clears the received date).
- **Rules** (`ExternalCaseRules`, pure): the status moves, which moves need a note, the date limits, tracked/overdue/wants-task (a case due today wants its task; the day after it is overdue), the 09:00 practice-time task moment.
- **History and audit:** every change appends a history entry (with who, the status before and after, the note) and an audit entry (ids and status names only) in the same save; the history is append-only in the database.
- **Documents linked, not copied:** link and unlink existing documents of the case's own patient; a link whose document became inactive, lost its file or failed its integrity check is listed with the reason; a finished case takes no link changes.
- **Task integration:** a durable ALV-N002 scheduler and two handlers make one unassigned system task (`ReferralFollowUp`, `LabCaseDue`) when a case that has gone out reaches its due date, High priority when already past due, and close it as the system when the case is completed, cancelled, back to a draft or given another due date; it appears in the work queue with a link to the case.
- **API:** `ExternalCasesController` (parties; the case list with filters, counts and paging; a patient timeline; create, read, edit, move, history, documents) with `ViewExternalCases`, `ManageExternalCases`, `ManageExternalParties`; a case's documents also need `ViewDocuments`.
- **UI:** Referrals and lab cases list, case detail, a patient "Referrals & labs" tab (cases, new referral and new lab case forms, timeline), Outside parties admin page; the work queue shows "Open the case" on a chase task. Statuses, kinds and OVERDUE are said in words.

## Decisions and deviations
The plan was put to the owner and approved before building: (A) the chase task is made when a case reaches its due date, not at creation, so the queue only shows what can be acted on; (B) referrals and lab cases are separate aggregates sharing only the party and document-link tables; (C) three new permissions rather than reusing the follow-up ones. Other assumptions: a lab case reaches its procedure through its treatment-plan item (no separate procedure column); hygienists and assistants can read cases but not change them; a finished case takes no link changes; a chase job that fails all its attempts is left `Failed` for triage (the same behaviour as the recall jobs).

## Defects found by own verification
- Negative controls: 2 of 33 survived the first pass (the `ViewDocuments` check on the documents routes, which no API test could tell because every role holding the case permissions also holds the document permission); `ExternalCaseControllerPermissionTests` added, 33 of 33 killed now.

- A schema test assumed the "both cases" check constraint answers before the unique link index; the index answers first for a row that breaks both (tests use a fresh document).
- The identity contract of this story is derived from the source and cross-checked against `--list-tests` (the finding of ALV-009-C01's R01, whose allowlist had missed a class).
