## The review finding and its correction (R02)
**Finding ALV-008-C02-R01-01.** `ChartMismatchesAsync` picked, for each completion, the finding version with the greatest `VersionNumber` among those whose `OccurredAtUtc <= FinalizedAtUtc`. The completion's chart write and a later legitimate chart change both take their time from the practice clock; when they share an instant (a fixed clock, or a clock whose resolution lets two operations tie) the later version satisfies `<=`, wins by number, and gives a false `chartMismatch`. Binding correction C2 asked for durable or deterministically derived evidence, not a timestamp approximation.

**Reproduced first.** `CompletionReconciliationTests.A_later_legitimate_chart_change_at_the_same_practice_clock_instant_leaves_an_adopted_completion_clear` adopts under a fixed clock, withdraws the finding at the same instant (the test asserts the tie is real) and failed on the R01 code with `chartMismatch` 1 instead of 0.

**Correction.**
- **A durable evidence boundary.** `CompletedProcedures.ToothFindingVersionNumber` (new column, nullable, with a check that it needs a finding and is at least 1) records the exact finding version the completion wrote (`Updated`) or observed (`AlreadyCompleted`). Both the authoritative completion and adoption set it. The record stays immutable (the existing trigger is untouched).
- **How the version is obtained.** The chart's moves and their history are now saved before the record is added (the transaction, the rollback and every other guarantee are unchanged), so the record can name a version that exists; `OdontogramService.CompletedVersionsAsync` then reads each matched finding's latest version inside the transaction and requires it to be an active Completed state. A finding this operation moved is held by its own row lock; one it only observed that was changed (for example withdrawn) between resolution and recording is refused with `chart_changed` (409) and nothing is stored, rather than recording wrong evidence. Two procedures on one finding in one set share the version the first one wrote.
- **Reconciliation** judges a completion against its recorded version alone: a mismatch is a missing version, a version that does not exist for that finding, one that does not show an active Completed state, or one newer than the completion. No timestamp chooses a version.
- **The database enforces it too.** Migration `AddCompletionChartVersion` (`20261013170209`) adds trigger `TR_CompletedProcedures_ChartEvidence` (error **51173**; **51174 and up are free**): a new record that names a finding must name a version that exists for it, shows it as an active Completed state and is not newer than the completion.
- **Existing completions (backfill, once).** A record made before the column existed is given, by the migration, the first active Completed version of its finding at or before the instant it was finalized. A finding only moves forward into Completed, so that is the version the completion wrote (or the earliest it could have observed), and a later change at the same instant, a link or a withdrawal cannot displace it. The timestamp is only an upper bound there; it never chooses among versions. A completion with no such version stays empty and reconciliation reports it. The table's immutability trigger is disabled for that one statement and enabled again. The rollback drops the trigger, the check and the column (the evidence is derived, so rolling forward restores it by the same rule).

**Proof (the four cases the review required, and more).** `CompletionChartEvidenceTests` (22 tests): (1) an adoption that writes a completed version records exactly that version and reconciles clean; (2) an adoption that observes an already-completed finding records the observed version and reconciles clean; (3) a later legitimate change at the same practice-clock instant leaves it clear (in both the `Updated` and the `AlreadyCompleted` case, and in the reproducing test above); (4) missing, non-completed, non-existent, withdrawn, newer-than-completion and altered-history evidence are each reported as `chartMismatch`. Also: the authoritative completion records the version (including two procedures on one finding); the database refuses each bad form of evidence on its own (51173) and the check constraints; the read refuses a finding that is not an active Completed state; the backfill is proved by a rollback and roll-forward on a history where "first", "latest" and "after the completion" give different answers, and by two cases where nothing can be derived. 12 negative controls, all killed (two survivors of the first pass were missing tests, now added).

## Decisions and deviations from the prompt's wording
- **The backfill is the only use of a timestamp** and is documented above; the reviewer asked that no timestamp-only approximation be used for reconciliation, and none is: after the migration every judgement is by the recorded version.
- **The evidence is stored on the record, not on the chart or in a separate table**, as the review suggested; the version is not a database foreign key (the evidence is checked by trigger 51173 at insert and by reconciliation afterwards), because adding an alternate key to the odontogram's version table would change a table this story does not own.
- **The extra save** (chart first, record second) was chosen over adding a foreign key so that EF's unordered inserts cannot put a record ahead of its evidence.

## Limitations / debt
- All R01 limitations stand (see `R01.md`), except that the reconciliation's chart evidence is now exact rather than inferred.
- **Completions made before this migration carry derived evidence** (the first active Completed version at or before the completion), not a version observed at the time; the rule is documented and its ties cannot change the answer, but it is a derivation. A completion for which none exists is reported as a mismatch.
- **There is no database foreign key to the version table** (see Decisions); trigger 51173 checks existence at insert, and the history versions are themselves immutable.
- **A finding changed between resolution and recording refuses the completion (`chart_changed`) instead of retrying;** the person reloads and tries again. It is deliberately conservative; it is covered by a unit test of the read, not by a deterministic race test of the whole operation.
- The mocked-browser suite has no spec for this change (it has no UI); the adoption walkthrough was rerun.
