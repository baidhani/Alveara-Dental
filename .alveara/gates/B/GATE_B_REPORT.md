## Disclosures

- **Judgement for the reviewer (B7).** The `STORY-004` handoff (inherited by `ALV-004-C01`) declares that availability is read before the locks are taken, so **blocked time added in the same instant as a booking may not be seen by that booking**. Provider, operatory and patient double-booking are locked and race-tested (six simultaneous requests give one appointment), so no double-booking, data loss, cross-patient exposure or history change results; the effect is that one appointment can land in time a manager blocked at that very instant, visible on the calendar and fixable by rescheduling. It is classed as not high-severity. If the reviewer rates it high, B7 fails and the responsible stories are `STORY-004` / `ALV-004-C01`.
- **The first version of the tablet probe reported false overflows** (301 px, 215 px, and 406 px on `/schedule` where the recorded widest element's right edge was 407 px) because it sampled while a page was being created, when the browser reports a tiny viewport width. The probe was corrected to require a real viewport width and the same overflow at two consecutive samples 150 ms apart, and a control test was added; the corrected probe and its control are what is reported. The first-version outputs were not retained; the full corrected runs are.
- **Tablet variants substitute one step.** At 768 px the open appointment drawer covers the shell's theme toggle, so in the calendar and flow variants the "switch theme" step sets the `data-theme` attribute and storage key the toggle sets instead of clicking it. Closing the drawer first is the user's path; no task is blocked.
- **Authentication timing.** The recovery-key step of `auth-real-backend` (a 5 s wait for RSA key generation) failed on two of three runs in the `ALV-N004` R06 attempt and on earlier attempts; it passed 4 of 4 here, on an idle machine, at default timing. The risk is not removed: under CPU load it can still time out. It is `ALV-N004`'s test and was not changed.
- **A load-sensitive unit test elsewhere.** While `ALV-N004` R06 stress-tested the backup test file, `src/FormTemplates.test.tsx` "creates a template with questions..." (`ALV-N010`) timed out at 5 s in two of three heavily loaded full-suite runs; it passed in every unloaded run, including this gate's (547/547). It is a test-timing sensitivity, not a product defect, and is reported for its own attention.
- **Commit identity.** The four `ALV-011-C01` SHAs the review names (`7923986`, `34d3ddf`, `a6b2905`, `17f6258`) were re-created by a rebase before first push; their on-main equivalents are `64d47bc`, `4313490`, `61c42e7`, `1c246cb` (identical content; mapping in `.alveara/BUILD_STATE.md`, commit `851a32b`). Every other reviewed SHA is an ancestor of this head.
- **Limits of the evidence.** Browser walkthroughs run against the Vite dev server proxying a real API (as for Gate A); axe scans are representative of the states exercised, not an exhaustive state enumeration; the mocked suites are not a substitute for the real-API walkthroughs and both are retained; the visit board refreshes by 15 s polling (an accepted policy, not push). Course completion for `STORY-003`, `STORY-004`, `STORY-011` remains portal-authoritative.
