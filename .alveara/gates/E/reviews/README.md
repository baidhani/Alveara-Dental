# Gate E reviews

The independent review of the Gate E Stage 1 package (`Alveara_Gate_E_c3d56e3.zip`) is stored here, unchanged, as `c3d56e3-stage1.md` once it arrives. The review of the Stage 2 regression package, and so the final gate decision, is stored as `c3d56e3.md`.

Stage 1 asks the reviewer to judge: the nine criteria against their predeclared thresholds (including the added row E9, Disclosure 1 of the report), the interpretation of "recoverable" for notifications (Disclosure 2), and the test-only repair `2be1d73` of the billing-ledger walkthrough (Disclosure 3). Gate E is not passed until Stage 2 has run and been reviewed.
