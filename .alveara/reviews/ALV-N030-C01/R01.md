## Independent findings

1. **Package integrity is valid.** Every packaged member listed in `MANIFEST.json` independently matches its recorded SHA-256. The implementation, evidence, and status SHAs are identified, the package is Stage 1 only, `releaseCertified` is false, and broad regression is not claimed.
2. **The owner invariant is enforced by the server.** Initial designation and the atomic staff-plus-owner path require an active, linked, enabled `Staff` account with role `Admin`; the acting designator is rechecked under lock inside the transaction. Role downgrade, profile inactivation, and login disable refuse the active owner with `owner_transfer_required`.
3. **Transfer follows the approved security contract.** It is limited to the enabled Deployment Administrator, requires password step-up and a current authenticator code when MFA is enabled, rejects recovery codes, carries `expectedDesignationId`, checks the destination under the documented lock order, moves the designation atomically, revokes the former owner's sessions, and optionally disables the former owner without changing their role. The idempotency fingerprint excludes credentials and includes the transfer intent.
4. **Recovery follows the approved C01 boundary.** Setup and recovery tokens are purpose-separated. Recovery is console-token based, rate-limited, available only in Normal mode, creates at most one Deployment Administrator or resets the existing one, rotates the security stamp, revokes sessions and recovery codes, turns MFA off, and does not introduce `MustEnrollMfa` or premature MFA enforcement. C02 remains the required QR-plus-real-authenticator gate before enforcement.
5. **Existing invalid owners are recorded, not silently promoted.** The migration and runtime helper share the deterministic append-only audit statement. The focused evidence covers repeat execution, a changed violation state, and a valid owner.
6. **Focused evidence is proportionate and honestly bounded.** The package records the one 5-minute-rule overrun and the three test-correction reruns. No owner walkthrough, broad backend suite, full client suite, accumulated chain, or release certification is implied.
