## R01 correction verified

R01 allowed an active completed/existing absence finding and an active ordinary finding to coexist, both through the Planned Missing → Caries → Missing Completed sequence and through concurrent writes. R02 closes that defect at the database boundary.

- Migration `AddToothPresenceInvariant` adds `TR_ToothFindings_ToothPresence`. For every affected patient/tooth it takes a transaction-owned exclusive application lock, then rejects both directions of the incompatible state: an ordinary finding on an absent tooth, or a finding that makes a tooth absent while ordinary findings are active.
- The trigger covers inserts and relevant state/status/condition updates, including writers outside the service layer. Its deterministic patient/tooth lock ordering avoids arbitrary lock ordering across a multi-row statement.
- Service prechecks provide stable, useful `409` responses (`tooth_absent`, `tooth_has_findings`, or bounded `tooth_busy`) while the trigger remains the authoritative concurrency guarantee.
- The chosen correction path is non-destructive refusal. It preserves clinical history and directs the clinician to withdraw obsolete findings with a reason before recording/completing the absence. Replacement findings remain allowed.
- Real-SQL test classes cover the exact R01 sequence, raw SQL, same-tooth concurrent insert and state-transition races, lock waiting, differing teeth, permitted replacements, legacy rows, and API behavior. The package’s mutation evidence shows these tests fail against the R01 behavior.
