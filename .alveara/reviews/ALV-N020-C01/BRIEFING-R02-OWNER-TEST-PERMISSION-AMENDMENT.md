# ALV-N020-C01 — Briefing R02 Owner Test-Permission Amendment

- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Applies to:** `.alveara/reviews/ALV-N020-C01/BRIEFING-R02.md`
- **Decision:** The product owner confirms the five-minute rule is a standing instruction, not an unapproved proposal.

The correction titled **“Remove the purported five-minute standing permission rule”** in `BRIEFING-R02.md` is withdrawn.

The briefing may retain its testing-permission approach, subject to the binding execution-plan policy: a focused group reasonably expected from documented history/command to finish in five minutes or less may run under the owner's standing permission; an unknown-duration group, a group expected to exceed five minutes, a chain/walkthrough expected to exceed five minutes, and every broad/full regression requires the owner's explicit permission before it starts. Evidence must record the permission route and expected/actual duration. If a standing-permission run materially exceeds its estimate, it must stop at a safe boundary rather than silently becoming a long run.

All other `BRIEFING-R02.md` corrections remain required. This amendment does not authorize implementation or test execution before the corrected pre-implementation briefing is independently approved.
