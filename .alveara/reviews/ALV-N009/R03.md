## Non-blocking observations and next-story impact

- The submitted repeated-request timeout test does not cross the renewal midpoint, so its comments overstate what it proves: immediate requests would also preserve expiry with sliding renewal enabled. The independent midpoint/expiry probe supplies the missing behavioral evidence for this approval. Retaining equivalent controlled-clock coverage in the repository would improve future regression protection.

This protects the completed sign-out transition, as the deterministic and browser checks confirm; the prose should not be used to infer cancellation at logout request initiation.
