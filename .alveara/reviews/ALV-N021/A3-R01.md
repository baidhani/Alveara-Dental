## Required corrections for R02

### CR-1 — Remove the second, unverified practice-zone source from Calendar and Flow Board

The approved briefing and inventory required `calendarLayout.practiceNow` to move into the shared practice-time arrangement **or** a testable invariant that the scheduling snapshot zone equals the authenticated session zone. R01 explicitly records neither: `INVENTORY_COMPARISON.md`, row 56, says the Calendar and Flow Board retain `snapshot.timeZoneId` and that the equality assertion was not added.

This is not merely an inventory difference. It leaves two clocks for what must be one practice-wide display rule: the authenticated-session zone used by the rest of the application and the scheduling-snapshot zone used to derive calendar/flow “today” and “now.” A configuration/response mismatch could therefore show inconsistent dates or time-sensitive controls.

For R02, choose one of these compliant outcomes:

1. preferred: make Calendar and Flow Board derive their display/today/now values through the approved shared practice-time helper using the authenticated session zone; or
2. retain the scheduling snapshot field only where needed for scheduling data, but add a hard, focused invariant at the response/client boundary that rejects or visibly fails a mismatch with `practiceTimeZoneId`, plus tests for matching and mismatching values.

Update the inventory comparison to record the final disposition. Do not quietly retain a second source without the invariant.

### CR-2 — Add the approved focused billing-document rendering proof

The approved A3 focused proof plan explicitly called for a statement-view component test that proves its displayed instants use the practice zone while the canonical document content remains unchanged. R01 instead says: “no dedicated new test; disclosed.” Disclosure does not replace the required proof.

For R02, add a focused `StatementView`/billing-document component test which:

- supplies an instant whose calendar date/time differs between at least two zones;
- sets the authenticated/render practice zone (and uses a browser zone that does not control the expectation);
- asserts the statement/receipt’s visible `asOfUtc` and at least one visible line/payment date display in the practice zone; and
- verifies document text/content other than the intended rendered time representation is not altered.

The test must exercise the component, not only `showInstant` in isolation.
