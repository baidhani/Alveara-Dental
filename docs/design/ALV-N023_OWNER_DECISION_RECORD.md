## Consequences the brief must address
1. **DR-1 is a schema change.** A plan item must be linkable to a real appointment (book from the item, show the appointment, follow a move or cancel), and only a linked, not-cancelled appointment or a terminal item state (completed, declined, withdrawn) may close the unscheduled-treatment follow-up. R05 PKG-08: "State-model change: escalate if schema is touched" - the brief states the schema design explicitly for the reviewer, with its migration, guards, rollback and the scheduling-module touch points (booking, move, cancel, no-show), and the effect on existing items marked Scheduled.
2. **PD-15 with DR-1:** the planned/target date stays a separate field from the booking; a past target date needs confirmation and shows "overdue"; it never looks like a booked appointment and never closes the follow-up.
3. **Existing data:** items already marked Scheduled (with or without a date, without an appointment) must be presented truthfully after the change (a target date, not a booking), and their follow-ups re-evaluated under the new rule; nothing recorded is rewritten.
4. **PD-14** adds a closed/terminal presentation (for example "Closed - no remaining procedures") distinct from Completed.
5. **DR-5** changes the rename API and form (a required reason) and the revision history wording ("Renamed from <old> to <new>" with the reason).
6. **DR-7**'s "resolved user names" applies to the plan audit detail text; the audit page's "Performed by" column showing ids is F-029 and stays with its own package unless the reviewer moves it.
