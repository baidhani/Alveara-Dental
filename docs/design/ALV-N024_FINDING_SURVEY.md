## Decisions for the owner (before the brief)
1. **PD-17 (F-114):** may a receipt or statement be reissued? Recommended: one receipt per payment; a replacement is deliberate, with a reason, a new number, the original marked "superseded by N", and an audit row. Statements are a cut-off, so a new statement at a later point is normal, not a reissue.
2. **PD-18 (F-209):** who may be recorded as the payer? Recommended: the patient, the registered guarantor and household members; anyone with the payment permission may choose; the payer is recorded in the ledger (as now).
3. **Q-5bf:** allow recording responsibility before the first charge? Recommended: yes (the server already does).
4. **F-193:** move payment methods off the patient tab now, or with the sub-tabs in ALV-N027? Recommended: with the sub-tabs (no half-move).
