| Id | Decision | Acceptance criterion | Attempt |
|---|---|---|---|

| OD-23 | Items: **Treatment ended** (always; the provider's End-treatment event); **Patient departed** (always; front desk records "Patient has left" with time); **Follow-up** (always); **Financial** (when applicable, OD-25). | Each item's open/resolved state is in the readiness response. | A2 |
| OD-24 | Follow-up outcomes: **booked** (detected automatically from a real future appointment for the patient), **not clinically required** (treating provider only), **patient declined** or **deferred with reason** (front desk). | Booked cannot be chosen by hand; it resolves only when a future Scheduled/Confirmed appointment exists. | A2 |

| OD-29 | The treating clinician's completion (OD-09) also needs every item resolved; it never bypasses front-desk or financial work. | Dentist or hygienist completion refused with open items. | A2 |
| OD-30 | Visits already in Ready for checkout when A2 ships go through the same gate (no grandfathering). | Legacy CheckedOut visit cannot complete until resolved. | A2 |
