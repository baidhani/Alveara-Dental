# ALV-N026 owner decision record

Decisions taken by the owner on 2026-11-20 after the read-only survey (`ALV-N026_FINDING_SURVEY.md`). The owner's words are quoted; nothing here is a reviewer decision. Each decision governs the pre-implementation brief. Where the owner asked for a design checkpoint, nothing in that area is coded before that checkpoint passes.

| Id | Decision | Owner's words |
|---|---|---|
| PD-24 (document visibility; Q-5cn, Q-5cl, F-173) | Clinical categories gated | "Add a security classification to each document category: clinical or non-clinical. Clinical documents require ViewClinicalDocumentation to list, open, or download. Non-clinical documents require ViewDocuments. Create separate ManageDocuments permission for edit/inactivate; ImportDocuments must not automatically grant it. Existing categories must be explicitly mapped. Any unknown/unreviewed category should default to clinical until classified, so it does not accidentally become broadly visible. Category reclassification must be audited. ManageDocuments does not bypass the appropriate read permission for document contents." |
| PD-11 (prescription content; F-110) | US federal/common baseline; research first | "ALV-N026 will research and implement a US federal/common baseline for prescription print content. Prescriber details must come from actual staff-profile data. The system must not invent identifiers, and it must not claim that a printed prescription is compliant with every US state or every other country. Controlled-substance and state-specific requirements remain outside this baseline unless independently researched and approved. Design the data model and print templates to avoid unnecessary permanent US-only labels where practical—for example, retain neutral concepts such as prescriber credential and issuing jurisdiction. This does not authorize a generic legal/compliance rules engine in ALV-N026. A separate, deferred engineering story for prescription jurisdiction profiles is required after ALV-N026 before the product is presented as supporting prescription baselines outside the selected US scope. That story must define reviewed, versioned, auditable country/region profiles; required credentials and identifiers; jurisdiction-specific print fields/notices; and controlled activation of approved profiles. Administrative customization may eventually cover presentation text and approved template options. It must not allow unrestricted editing of legal/compliance requirements or labeling an unreviewed configuration as compliant. The ALV-N026 pre-implementation briefing must identify the exact official US sources used, the fields implemented, and the excluded jurisdictional requirements. Any broader compliance claim requires independent review before code." |
| PD-25 (backup alert delivery; F-152) | In-app inbox and banner | "Send backup failures and restore results to every administrator's in-app inbox. Show a persistent administrator banner. Acknowledgment is audited, but System Status remains in warning until the underlying backup/restore condition is resolved. If notification creation fails, retain the durable alert record, existing file drop, and System Status warning. Defer email/SMTP delivery." |
| F-164 / Q-5aq (ended prescriptions) | Print a record copy with a watermark | "Keep the action as Print record copy for cancelled, discontinued, and superseded prescriptions. Apply an unmissable diagonal watermark such as CANCELLED — DO NOT DISPENSE. Include the status date and reason. Active prescriptions retain normal printing. Record-copy printing remains audit logged." |
| F-108 / F-109 (print method) | Server-side PDF; **a design checkpoint before adding the dependency** | "Browser print CSS cannot reliably force browsers to suppress their own headers/footers; it can improve layout, but it cannot guarantee that the internal patient URL will never print. For prescriptions, financial records, and clinical documents, exact output matters. Requirements: Generate the document as a server-side PDF; the browser prints/downloads that PDF rather than the application page. The agent must first propose the PDF library with its license, maintenance status, security posture, offline operation, and deterministic-output approach for approval. No external PDF service or patient-data upload. Preserve versioned templates and byte-for-byte rebuild compatibility for historical versions. This is an escalation/design checkpoint before adding the dependency." |
| D-06 (System Status) | All three sections, administrator-only | "Backup and recovery: last good backup, last restore drill, recovery-key configured yes/no, overdue state, and unresolved PD-25 alert status. Storage: document-folder reachability, free disk capacity, and missing/damaged-document counts. Staff accounts summary: enabled/disabled/locked totals and accounts without MFA. Keep the whole page administrator-only. Show counts/statuses only—no file paths, recovery keys, usernames, document names, or other sensitive details." |
| OQ-2 (MRN on printed materials; from ALV-N025) | All four | "Put the MRN on: Prescription; Treatment-plan patient copy; Statement; Receipt. Newly generated documents use the new template versions; existing historical documents must continue rebuilding with their original versions byte-for-byte. Do not backfill or silently re-version previously issued financial documents." |

## Consequences recorded for the brief
- **Two checkpoints before any related code:**
  - the PDF library proposal (licence, maintenance, security, offline, deterministic output), approved by the owner and independently reviewed as an escalation;
  - the PD-11 source research (the exact official US sources, the fields, the exclusions), independently reviewed.
- **A new deferred story** is required after ALV-N026: prescription jurisdiction profiles (PD-11).
- **New permission:** `ManageDocuments`. That is a permission-model change, so it is escalated with the brief.
- **New default:** Dentist gets `ImportDocuments`.
- **U-008 is assigned to ALV-N026** (Dentist only).
